BazelCon 2026 Recap: Recordings and Notes

By Karolina Kalin on 10 November 2026

Another edition of BazelCon has come to a close. We gathered to discuss what matters most in the world of Bazel, learn, network and spend some quality time with like-minded folks. If you couldn’t make it to the event or want a quick refresher on the key takeaways, we’ve put together a brief summary of the highlights.

TL;DR - Recordings playlist, photo album, user survey

Acknowledgments

The success of this year’s event is entirely due to our incredible community.

A huge thank you to our sponsors, whose support made it possible to host an event of this scale free of charge.

Thank you to our speakers and trainers for delivering insightful presentations packed with knowledge that sparked many conversations throughout the week.

And to our attendees - both in-person and online - for your questions, hallway discussions, and engagement that truly shape the spirit of BazelCon each year. We gathered a record number of 455 people, exceeding our expectations and giving us much to consider for next events.

BUILD Foundation

Kicking off Day 1 during the Keynote, Helen Altshuler and Chuck Grindel announced the launch of the BUILD Foundation. Governed by the Linux Foundation with initial members ASML, Canva, Datadog, EngFlow, Figma, and Spotify, the Foundation gives the Bazel ecosystem the leadership and sustained investment needed to take on challenges no single organization can solve alone.

Key priorities for the initiative include improving technical documentation, funding maintainers for bazel-contrib repositories, launching developer training and certification, and enhancing software supply chain security.

By formalizing this shared investment, the community aims to keep build times fast and dependable as engineering teams and codebases continue to scale.

To discover more about the launch, explore the initial member perspectives, and find out how your organization can get involved, read the rest of the Linux Foundation press release and watch the Keynote.
